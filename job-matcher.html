How to improve

    Improve my CV

    This is a keyword-overlap estimate to help you tailor your CV. It does not predict what any recruiter or ATS will decide. Only add skills you genuinely have.

    How is the score calculated?

    There is no AI model and no server involved. The page runs a simple, fixed set of rules on your device:

    • Skills (60%). We look for roughly 280 common skills, tools and languages in the job description (with alternative spellings such as “JS” for JavaScript). Each skill counts once. Skills under “preferred” or “nice to have” headings count half; skills mentioned more than once count 1.5×. The share found in your CV is your skills score.
    • Keywords (30%). We also pick the words and two-word phrases that repeat in the job post (ignoring common filler) and check whether your CV uses them.
    • Role words (10%). If you enter a job title, we check how many of its words appear in your CV.

    If a part cannot be measured (for example, no job title entered, or fewer than three repeated keywords in a short post), its weight is shared among the others. Word forms are compared loosely (“analysis” and “analyses”), but meaning is not: “team lead” and “manager” are different words to this tool.

    Privacy. Your saved CV is read from this browser. Uploaded files and the job description stay on your device. PDF and Word files are read by open-source libraries loaded from a public CDN the first time you upload one.
